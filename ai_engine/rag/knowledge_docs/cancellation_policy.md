# SupportIQ Order Cancellation Policy

> **DEMONSTRATION CONTENT ONLY**: This document is a synthetic sample policy created for SupportIQ testing and demonstration purposes. It does not represent any real company policy, legal terms, or actual customer data.

## 1. Cancellation Cut-Off Window
Customers may cancel an order or request an order cancellation immediately after checkout before the order ships. Because our automated fulfillment centers prioritize rapid dispatch, orders move swiftly through fulfillment stages:
- **Eligible Statuses**: Orders currently in `ACTIVE`, `PAID`, or `PROCESSING` status can be cancelled instantly without penalty.
- **Immediate Reversal**: Upon cancellation, reserved inventory is returned to warehouse stock and payments are reversed to the original payment source.

## 2. Ineligible & Restricted Cancellation Statuses
Orders that have progressed to the shipping stage cannot be cancelled directly via the automated interface:
- **Restricted Statuses**: If an order status is marked as `SHIPPED`, `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`, or `DELIVERED`, direct cancellation is restricted.
- **Courier In-Transit Procedure**: Once items are with the courier, customers must wait for delivery and initiate an authorized Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) under our Refund Policy.
- **Interception Requests**: High-priority interception requests for freight shipments may incur a $25 carrier rerouting fee.

## 3. Pre-Order & Backordered Item Cancellations
- Backordered items or pre-orders can be cancelled at any time prior to inventory allocation and shipping label generation.
- No restocking fee is charged for backordered item cancellations.

## 4. Partial Order Cancellations
- If an order contains multiple items and only a portion has been dispatched, the remaining unshipped line items may be cancelled.
- Any bundled volume discounts or threshold coupons will be recalculated based on the remaining items in the order.
