# SupportIQ Payment & Billing FAQ

> **DEMONSTRATION CONTENT ONLY**: This document is a synthetic sample policy created for SupportIQ testing and demonstration purposes. It does not represent any real company policy, legal terms, or actual customer data.

## 1. What payment methods are accepted?
SupportIQ supports all major payment methods across web and mobile platforms:
- Major Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover).
- Digital Wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal).
- Buy Now Pay Later (Klarna, Affirm) for eligible orders over $100.
- SupportIQ Store Credit and Electronic Gift Cards.
We do not accept cash-on-delivery (COD), personal bank checks, or cryptocurrency.

## 2. When is my card charged?
Your payment method is authorized at the moment you click "Place Order" and charged when the order begins fulfillment processing at our warehouse. For backordered items or pre-orders, authorization holds may be refreshed periodically until dispatch.

## 3. How do promo codes and coupons work with refunds?
- Promo codes and digital discounts apply proportionally across eligible line items.
- If you return an item purchased with a promotional bundle discount, your refund will reflect the discounted price paid, not the full retail value.
- Once an order using a single-use coupon is cancelled or refunded, the coupon code cannot be reactivated or reissued.

## 4. What happens if my payment fails?
If a payment transaction fails during checkout:
- Verify that billing address zip code matches the credit card statement address.
- Ensure two-factor 3D-Secure authentication has been confirmed via your mobile banking app.
- If an authorization hold appears on your bank statement for a declined order, your financial institution will automatically release the hold within 2 to 5 business days.

## 5. How do I dispute an unrecognized charge?
Before filing a formal chargeback with your credit card issuer, we strongly recommend reaching out to SupportIQ Customer Care. Filing an unverified bank chargeback automatically locks the associated customer account pending security investigation, which can delay refunds by up to 45 calendar days.
