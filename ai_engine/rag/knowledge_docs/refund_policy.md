# SupportIQ Customer Refund Policy

> **DEMONSTRATION CONTENT ONLY**: This document is a synthetic sample policy created for SupportIQ testing and demonstration purposes. It does not represent any real company policy, legal terms, or actual customer data.

## 1. Overview & Eligibility Window
SupportIQ customers are eligible to return an item or request a refund for eligible products within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date. Items must be in their original packaging, unused, and include all original accessories, documentation, and tags.

## 2. Refund Approval Tiers & Thresholds
To ensure fair and transparent operations while preventing fraudulent behavior, refunds are categorized into the following approval tiers:
- **Tier 1 (Automated Refund - Up to $1,000)**: Requests with an order value less than or equal to $1,000 are eligible for automatic evaluation and instant execution if customer history meets standard risk criteria.
- **Tier 2 (Manager Approval - $1,001 to $5,000)**: Requests between $1,001 and $5,000 require manual review and sign-off by a customer support manager. Processing time is typically 1 to 2 business days.
- **Tier 3 (Executive / Fraud Review - Over $5,000)**: High-value refund requests exceeding $5,000 require executive authorization and mandatory validation from the fraud prevention department.

## 3. Customer Risk Controls & Frequency Limits
- Customers with **more than 2 previous refunds** within a rolling 90-day window are flagged for manual verification before any new refund is approved.
- Accounts with **3 or more unresolved complaints** or flagged status are ineligible for automated refunds and will be routed directly to senior escalation agents.

## 4. Refund Processing Time & Payment Method
- Approved refunds will be credited back to the original method of payment within **3 to 5 business days**.
- In the event that the original payment method is expired or cancelled, the refund will be issued as non-expiring SupportIQ Store Credit upon identity verification.
- Promotional discounts and coupons applied at checkout are non-refundable and will be deducted from the calculated refund balance.

## 5. Non-Refundable Items & Exceptions
The following items are strictly non-refundable:
- Downloadable digital software licenses and digital gift cards.
- Clearance and final-sale merchandise marked "AS-IS".
- Perishable goods or personalized custom-engraved products.
- Shipping fees and expedited delivery surcharges, unless the return is caused by a courier transit error or warehouse defect.
