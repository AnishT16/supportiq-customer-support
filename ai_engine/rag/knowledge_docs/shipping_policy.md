# SupportIQ Shipping & Delivery Policy

> **DEMONSTRATION CONTENT ONLY**: This document is a synthetic sample policy created for SupportIQ testing and demonstration purposes. It does not represent any real company policy, legal terms, or actual customer data.

## 1. Shipping Methods & Estimated Delivery Timelines
SupportIQ partners with regional and international logistics providers to offer three primary shipping tiers:
- **Standard Ground Shipping**: Delivery within **3 to 5 business days**. Complimentary for domestic orders exceeding $50.
- **Expedited Shipping**: Delivery within **2 business days** when ordered before 2:00 PM EST. Flat rate fee of $15.
- **Priority Overnight Shipping**: Guaranteed next-business-day delivery by 12:00 PM local time. Flat rate fee of $35.

## 2. Order Tracking & Carrier Updates
- A unique tracking number and live carrier URL are generated within 12 hours of package dispatch.
- Tracking milestones include: `Package Accepted`, `In Transit`, `Out for Delivery`, and `Delivered`.
- Carrier tracking numbers may take up to 24 hours after dispatch to show initial scan activity at the origin distribution hub.

## 3. Shipping Delays & Proactive Notifications
- If a delivery delay exceeds **3 business days** beyond the estimated delivery date, SupportIQ's predictive engine flags the shipment for proactive intervention.
- Impacted customers will receive an automated email and SMS notification detailing the delay reason (such as severe weather, warehouse backlog, or carrier routing bottlenecks).
- For delayed shipments exceeding 5 business days, customers are eligible for a full refund of shipping charges or a complimentary re-order item dispatch.

## 4. Damaged or Lost Packages in Transit
- In the event of a package lost in transit (no tracking updates for 7 consecutive business days), SupportIQ will initiate a carrier trace investigation.
- If the carrier declares the parcel lost, a replacement shipment will be dispatched immediately at zero additional expense to the customer.
- Physical damage incurred during shipping must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence of package and item condition.
