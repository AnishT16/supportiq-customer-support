# SupportIQ Product Warranty Policy

> **DEMONSTRATION CONTENT ONLY**: This document is a synthetic sample policy created for SupportIQ testing and demonstration purposes. It does not represent any real company policy, legal terms, or actual customer data.

## 1. Standard Limited Warranty Coverage
All hardware devices and electronic products sold through SupportIQ include a **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** effective from the original date of retail delivery. This warranty guarantees that products are free from defects in materials and manufacturing workmanship under normal consumer use.

## 2. Warranty Exclusions & Limitations
The limited warranty does not cover issues resulting from:
- Accidental drops, physical impact, liquid damage, or extreme temperature exposure.
- Unauthorized disassembly, hardware tampering, third-party software rooting, or modifications.
- Consumable parts subject to natural wear and tear (such as disposable batteries, screen protectors, cables, cosmetic cosmetic scuffs).
- Products purchased from unauthorized third-party resellers or auction platforms.

## 3. Warranty Claim & RMA Procedure
To file a warranty claim:
1. Contact customer support or submit an automated ticket citing the original order ID and device serial number.
2. Provide a clear description of the defect and diagnostic error messages.
3. Once validated, support generates a prepaid Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) shipping label.
4. Upon inspection at the service depot (usually completed within 48 hours of receipt), SupportIQ will either repair the defect, issue a certified replacement device, or provide store credit.

## 4. Extended Warranty Protection Plan
Customers can purchase the **SupportIQ Care+ Extended Protection Plan** within 30 days of initial purchase. This extends warranty coverage to 3 full years and includes up to 2 incidents of accidental damage handling per year subject to a nominal $49 service deductible.
